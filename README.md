# MarSan India Cost Clarity — public previews

Share the [BOT & talent partner comparison](https://ankurjal1978-ai.github.io/marsan-india-cost-clarity-preview/bot-switch-demo.html) or [India supplier clarity](https://ankurjal1978-ai.github.io/marsan-india-cost-clarity-preview/ops-simplifier-demo.html) with testers. Both work without login or email and save no records.

These previews use planning scenarios. The 20% BOT and 12% staff augmentation rates are editable starting assumptions, not verified market averages or claims about named competitors. The 10% and 8% MarSan rates are planning inputs and require a final commercial proposal. The independent-operation tool does not claim cash savings without an actual consolidated fee proposal.

The production lead-capture backend is supplied separately to MarSan's web team and is not hosted in this repository. See [research basis](./RESEARCH.md).
