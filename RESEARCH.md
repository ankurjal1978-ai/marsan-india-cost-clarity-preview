# Research and messaging basis

This research guides questions and positioning; it does not establish competitor prices.

- [ANSR's BOT overview](https://ansr.com/build-operate-transfer/) describes Build–Operate–Transfer as an operating model with recruitment, infrastructure, compliance/governance and eventual transfer. This supports asking about current BOT spend, transfer readiness and contract timing. The assessment does not state ANSR's rates.
- [EY on GCC operating models](https://www.ey.com/en_in/insights/consulting/global-capability-centers/why-gcc-operating-models-offer-a-new-competitive-advantage) links governance, talent, facilities, service delivery and partners. This supports treating operating complexity as a leadership issue alongside cost.
- [EY's Capability Center as a Service description](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/consulting/capability-center-as-a-service) spans payroll, tax/compliance, facilities/IT vendor coordination and governance. This supports the independent-operation fee categories.
- [Deel's India EOR overview](https://www.deel.com/blog/employer-of-record-india/) distinguishes salary/statutory employer costs from an EOR management fee. The supplier calculator therefore excludes wages and pass-through costs from service-fee savings.

**Commercial assumptions:** 20% current BOT markup and 12% current staff augmentation markup were provided as planning starting points by MarSan. We did not find a reliable, comparable public industry benchmark that would support presenting them as market facts. Users can edit them. MarSan 10% BOT and 8% staff augmentation are scenario inputs supplied by MarSan, subject to a final scope and proposal. No competitor-specific price claim is made.

**Campaign hook:** “Already in India? Know what your delivery model really costs.” The BOT path appeals to leaders who already have an outsourced partner; the supplier path appeals to leaders carrying multiple service contracts and invoices. Each delivers a useful planning answer before requesting a sales conversation. The public testing previews collect nothing.
